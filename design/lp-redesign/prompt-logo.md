# Prompt de logo — Vetrium (para gerar variações no ChatGPT)

Colar no ChatGPT (modelo de imagem). Pedir 4 variações por vez; repetir
trocando a linha "Direção" para explorar caminhos diferentes.

```
Design a logo for "Vetrium", a small Brazilian design and web studio that builds
landing pages for local businesses (barbershops, clinics, service providers).
The brand should feel creative, modern and approachable — quality work at an
accessible price, not luxury, not corporate, not gamer.

Deliver: a simple symbol (monogram based on the letter V) plus the lowercase
wordmark "vetrium" next to it.

Visual language (must match the website):
- Flat vector shapes only: no 3D, no bevels, no metallic effects, no neon glow,
  no gradients, no shadows.
- Built from rounded, pill-like forms and clean geometry; consistent stroke
  weight; generous negative space.
- Wordmark set in a tight, modern grotesque sans-serif (similar to Inter Tight),
  medium weight, slightly tight letter spacing.
- Colors: deep charcoal #0B0D12 and off-white #F2F3F6, with a single accent of
  electric violet #6C3BFF used sparingly.
- Must stay recognizable at 16x16 px (favicon) and work in one color.

Direção: [escolha uma]
- "the V is formed by two pill shapes meeting at the bottom"
- "the V turns into an up-right arrow, suggesting growth and results"
- "the V is drawn on a modular grid of rounded squares"
- "the v is an italic serif letter inside a circle, contrasting with a sans wordmark"
- "free exploration: surprise me, keeping the constraints above"

Show each logo on a dark charcoal background and on an off-white background,
centered, with lots of empty space. No mockups, no extra text, no taglines.
```

Dica: quando gostar de uma, peça "make 4 refined variations of option N,
same constraints" e me mande a imagem. Eu redesenho em vetor de verdade
(SVG limpo), gero favicon e aplico no site.
